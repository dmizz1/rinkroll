    Fan-made game. Rink Roll is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National Hockey League, the NHL Players' Association, or any team. Player names and statistics are used for reference only.
    How the grades work. Current players are ranked against everyone at their position, then curved: roughly the top 2% earn S and the bottom 8% get F. Speed blends max skating speed and 20+ mph bursts. Shot blends shot velocity, goal rate and shooting %. Hands, Sense and Playmaking use finishing from the slot, offensive-zone time, points, assists and power-play production. Build is height and weight. Defense adds Physicality (size, penalty minutes, shot power) and Stick-checking (ice time, plus/minus, clean play). Goalie grades come from save % by shot distance, three-season save %, and career save %. Small samples are pulled toward average.
    Public NHL data doesn't split saves by glove or blocker side, so goalies are graded on save % by shot distance instead: Mid-range and Long-range. All-Time Greats covers players whose careers fall mostly between 1980 and 2026 (100 forwards, 100 defensemen, 35 goalies), graded by hand from each player's career and reputation.
    Current mode only includes players with at least 82 career NHL regular-season games, and forwards need at least 0.30 career points per game. Data: NHL rosters, stats and EDGE tracking updated November 6, 2026.
    Head-to-head connects the two browsers directly through PeerJS. No accounts, and nothing is stored on a server. How to play · Privacy policy
