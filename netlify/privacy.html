Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Rink Roll is a free hockey game that runs in your browser. This page explains what information is involved when you play, and the choices you have.

What we store

Rink Roll has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. The game saves a few settings in your own browser's local storage so they are there next time:

  • whether sound is on or off,
  • the display name you type for head-to-head games,
  • your progress and result for today's Daily challenge,
  • random IDs that let your device rejoin a head-to-head lobby and post one leaderboard entry per day.

This information stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

Head-to-head games and the leaderboard

When you play head-to-head, the lobby is stored on this site's server so both players can see it: the lobby settings, the display name you typed, your build progress, and a random ID that lets your device rejoin the lobby. When you post a Daily build to the leaderboard, the name you typed and your build are shown publicly, along with a random device ID that is kept private and used only to stop duplicate entries. No other personal information is collected.
