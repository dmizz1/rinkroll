- Average build stat: overall, by mode and by position
- Real global ranking: all-time, by average Daily grade with a minimum (e.g. 10 Dailies); account holders only
- Online H2H record (wins/losses when both players are signed in), maybe a chess-style rating

- Undecided: sign-in method (recommended: email link sign-in; alternatives: Google, username + password)
