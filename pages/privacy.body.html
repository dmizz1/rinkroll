Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Rink Roll is a free hockey game that runs in your browser. This page explains what information is involved when you play, and the choices you have.

What we store

Rink Roll has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. The game saves a few settings in your own browser's local storage so they are there next time:

  • whether sound is on or off,
  • the display name you type for head-to-head games,
  • your progress and result for today's Daily challenge.

This information stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

Head-to-head games

Head-to-head uses PeerJS, a free service that helps two browsers find each other and then connects them directly. To make that connection, your browser shares technical information such as your IP address with the PeerJS connection server and with the other player's browser. The only game information sent is the lobby settings, your display name and your build progress. Nothing is stored on our side after the game ends.

Rink Roll is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
