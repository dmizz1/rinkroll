Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Rink Roll is a free hockey game that runs in your browser. This page explains what information is involved when you play, and the choices you have.

What we store

You can play Rink Roll without an account, and then it does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. The game saves a few settings in your own browser's local storage so they are there next time:

  • whether sound is on or off,
  • the display name you type for head-to-head games,
  • your progress and result for today's Daily challenge,
  • random IDs that let your device rejoin a head-to-head lobby and post one leaderboard entry per day.

This information stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

Accounts (optional)

If you choose to make an account, we store:

  • your email address, used only to sign you in and to send sign-in and password-reset links (we never send marketing email),
  • the display name you pick and the profile name made from it,
  • your password, if you set one, stored only as a scrambled hash that nobody can read back,
  • if you sign in with Google, the Google account ID and email address Google shares with us (we do not get your Google password or anything else from your Google account),
  • your game history and stats: finished builds, Daily results and streaks, Line Builder seasons, head-to-head results, badges and the badges you pick to show.

While you're signed in, a sign-in cookie keeps you signed in on that browser for up to 60 days, or until you sign out. Your display name, profile, badges, stats and recent builds can be seen by anyone, for example on the global ranking and at your profile link. Your email address is never shown publicly.

Sign-in and password-reset emails are sent through Resend, an email delivery service. Accounts are only for people 13 or older.

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings tab on your account page. That permanently removes your account, email address, stats and badges. Daily leaderboard entries you already posted stay on that day's leaderboard under the name you used.

Head-to-head games and the leaderboard

When you play head-to-head, the lobby is stored on this site's server so both players can see it: the lobby settings, the display name you typed, your build progress, and a random ID that lets your device rejoin the lobby. When you post a Daily build to the leaderboard, the name you typed and your build are shown publicly, along with a random device ID that is kept private and used only to stop duplicate entries. If you're signed in, your entry also shows your profile name and showcase badges, and your account is linked to the entry privately to award the Top of the Board badge. No other personal information is collected.

Children

Rink Roll is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Accounts are only for people 13 or older, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.
